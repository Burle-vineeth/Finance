Five parts, each scored out of 100 and weighted. Each gives partial points: they fall evenly from full marks at its best to none at its worst. A part with nothing to measure is left out, and the rest scaled to 100.

  • Collections (30): payments due in the period, installments and late fees, made within {{ d.settings.graceDays }} days. 100% scores full marks, 0% none.
  • Overdue risk (25): money overdue on the last day, as a share of the money lent out then. None overdue scores full marks, 50% or more none.
  • Earnings (20): the interest the lent money earned in the period, after expenses, as a yearly rate. {{ d.settings.targetYearlyRate }}% a year or more scores full marks, 0% or less none. Each payment counts its loan's share of interest.
  • Money at work (15): how much of the money in the business was lent out rather than sitting as cash, on average. 90% or more scores full marks, nothing lent none.
  • Spread of risk (10): how much of the money lent sits with the biggest borrower on the last day. 25% or less scores full marks, everything with one borrower none.

80 and over is Strong, 60 Good, 40 Needs attention, below that At risk. A short range, like a week, swings more: one payment more or less moves it a lot. Past periods use each loan's current terms, so a loan edited later counts with its new schedule.
